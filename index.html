مراحل الفلتر

Pre-Filter Stage

  1. وظيفتها إيه؟
    • First line of defense that removes sand, rust, dust, and large particles to protect the rest of the filter stages.
  2. الفايدة الأساسية؟
    • Protects inner stages from early clogging and extends the full system lifespan.

Carbon Filter Stage

  1. وظيفتها إيه؟
    • Activated carbon absorbs chlorine and chemical residues, improving taste and odor while protecting the RO membrane from chlorine damage.
  2. الفايدة الأساسية؟
    • Improves water flavor and smell while reducing chemical load before deep filtration.

Third Stage

  1. وظيفتها إيه؟
    • A tighter carbon polishing stage that removes remaining chlorine traces and fine organic compounds before water reaches the membrane.
  2. الفايدة الأساسية؟
    • Prepares water for RO with cleaner input and better filtration efficiency.

RO Membrane Stage

  1. وظيفتها إيه؟
    • The RO membrane performs deep filtration, reducing salts, heavy metals, and microorganisms to deliver highly purified drinking water.
  2. الفايدة الأساسية؟
    • Provides the strongest purification stage and significantly lowers dissolved contaminants.

Final Carbon Stage

  1. وظيفتها إيه؟
    • Post-carbon treatment refines flavor after tank storage and removes any residual storage odor for a cleaner final taste.
  2. الفايدة الأساسية؟
    • Delivers a smoother drinking taste and cleaner smell at the final output.

Mineral Stage

  1. وظيفتها إيه؟
    • Re-mineralization restores useful minerals like calcium and magnesium to balance taste and support healthy daily hydration.
  2. الفايدة الأساسية؟
    • Balances water profile after RO and improves daily drinking quality.

Final Sterilization Stage

  1. وظيفتها إيه؟
    • UV sterilization provides a final safety layer by neutralizing remaining microbes without adding chemicals.
  2. الفايدة الأساسية؟
    • Adds extra microbiological safety for more reliable drinking water.
